Scale: 0-100 per aspect. Higher = more documented problems. 0 = nothing documented.

## Demonetisation 2016
- outcome_failure: 75  — RBI's own report said about 99.3% of withdrawn Rs 500/1000 notes came back, which undercuts the black-money aim; cash in circulation later returned to pre-2016 levels.
- cost_waste: 60  — new-note printing and handling costs rose sharply in 2016-17 per RBI reporting; wider output losses are estimated but disputed.
- transparency_gap: 55  — announced with about four hours notice; RBI board reservations only surfaced later through RTI releases.
- ground_level_harm: 70  — long queues, reported deaths (counts disputed), and income and job losses in the cash-dependent informal sector.
- legal_challenges: 35  — challenged in the Supreme Court; the 2023 judgment upheld it 4 to 1, with one judge dissenting on procedure.
- equity_impact: 65  — cash-dependent poor households, small traders and farmers bore a larger share of the burden.

## Agnipath Scheme 2022
- outcome_failure: 40  — early to judge; the plan to keep only a quarter of recruits and recent reports of a revised version suggest the design is still being adjusted.
- cost_waste: 30  — short tenures mean training investment is not recovered over a full career; savings claims and costs are not clearly documented.
- transparency_gap: 50  — introduced by executive decision in 2022 without parliamentary debate, and revised after protests.
- ground_level_harm: 55  — violent protests in June 2022 and lasting uncertainty for recruits about life after four years.
- legal_challenges: 30  — challenges were filed; the Delhi High Court upheld the scheme in 2023.
- equity_impact: 50  — differences in pension and benefits between Agniveers and regular soldiers.

## Farm laws 2020
- outcome_failure: 85  — repealed in November 2021 without being fully implemented.
- cost_waste: 30  — little documented direct spending; main cost was the year of disruption.
- transparency_gap: 80  — passed as ordinances then by voice vote in the Rajya Sabha amid protest, with no committee scrutiny.
- ground_level_harm: 70  — year-long protests, many farmer deaths reported by unions (government disputed the figures), and violence at Lakhimpur Kheri.
- legal_challenges: 65  — the Supreme Court stayed implementation in January 2021 and appointed a committee.
- equity_impact: 45  — concerns about marginal farmers, MSP and corporate bargaining power, which supporters disputed.
